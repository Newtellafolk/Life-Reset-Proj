# Companion Rulebook

Status: ready-for-human

Author and review the written rules for what the Companion will and will not do: no diagnosis or treatment claims, no directive financial/relationship/medical advice, how it declines gracefully, when it refers out, and how it speaks (non-judgmental, no lecturing). A product owner decides the content; an agent can draft a first version for review. Required before any LLM-backed test.

Acceptance:
- A document in the repo, reviewed and approved, referenced from the LLM adapter's instructions.
- Includes example good and bad replies for money, relationship, work and family problems.
- Consistent with ADR-0001 and the Safety Net.

Blocked by: none.
