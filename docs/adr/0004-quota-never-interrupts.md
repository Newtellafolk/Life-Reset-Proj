# The monthly quota never interrupts a Session or gates essential help

The quota counts Sessions, not messages, and may only block the start of a new Session. A Session in progress is never cut off or interrupted by a quota prompt, and the Grounding Exercise, the Safety Net, and reading one's own History are never gated. The prototype's earlier behavior (a modal opening mid-conversation at message five) would land on people at their most vulnerable and contradicts the product's promise of a non-judgmental, non-pressuring space; it would also let a paywall stand between a person and crisis resources. We accept the cost of a looser, harder-to-meter quota in exchange for never being the reason someone is cut off. The quota value itself stays a placeholder until real usage informs it.
