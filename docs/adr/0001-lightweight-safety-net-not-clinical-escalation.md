# Lightweight Safety Net instead of clinical escalation

Life Reset is positioned as a wellness/coaching companion, not a mental-health treatment product — it must never diagnose, treat, or claim to replace a licensed therapist. Given that boundary, when the Companion detects crisis-indicating language, it surfaces the 1323 mental health hotline as a referral (the Safety Net) rather than building a clinical escalation system (e.g. live triage, mandated reporting, in-house crisis counselors). We chose the lighter mechanism because a clinical escalation system would implicitly claim clinical capability and liability the product does not have the licensing or staffing to back up, and would require a completely different regulatory and operational posture. A future reader should not "fix" the Safety Net into a full escalation system without first revisiting the product's clinical/wellness boundary.
